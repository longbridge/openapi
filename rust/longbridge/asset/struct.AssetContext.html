pub async fn bank_cards(&self) -> Result<Vec<BankCard>>

Get the linked bank cards, with their verification, binding and authorization (eDDA, bank securities account, FPS) status

AP accounts only: US data-center accounts get a region error.

Path: GET /v2/account/bank-cards

§Examples
use std::sync::Arc;

use longbridge::{Config, asset::AssetContext, oauth::OAuthBuilder};

let oauth = OAuthBuilder::new("your-client-id")
    .build(|url| println!("Visit: {url}"))
    .await?;
let config = Arc::new(Config::from_oauth(oauth));
let ctx = AssetContext::new(config);

for card in ctx.bank_cards().await? {
    println!("{} {:?}", card.bank, card.status);
}

pub async fn reward_records( &self, options: impl Into<Option<GetRewardRecordsOptions>>, ) -> Result<RewardRecordsResponse>

Get reward records from the reward center (cash, gifts, coupons, task rewards)

The route has no date filter: to find rewards in a time range, page through the records and filter on their times.

AP accounts only: US data-center accounts get a region error.

Path: GET /v1/account/rewards

pub async fn coupons( &self, options: impl Into<Option<GetCouponsOptions>>, ) -> Result<CouponsResponse>

Get coupons (reward cards)

By default lists the coupons that have not expired; see GetCouponsOptions::expired. The pinned top_list / bottom_list are repeated on every page.

AP accounts only: US data-center accounts get a region error.

Path: GET /v1/account/cards

pub async fn coupon_orders( &self, card_id: impl Into<String>, order_type: CouponOrderType, ) -> Result<Vec<CouponOrder>>

Get the orders a coupon was applied to

card_id is crate::asset::Coupon::card_id (32 hex characters, not a bank card ID). The gateway rate-limits this route tightly per member, so avoid calling it concurrently.

AP accounts only: US data-center accounts get a region error.

Path: GET /v1/account/card-orders

Source

pub async fn deposits( &self, options: impl Into<Option<GetDepositsOptions>>, ) -> Result<DepositsResponse>

Get deposit records

The server’s total follows the page size rather than the record count, so it is not returned; DepositsResponse::has_more reports a full page instead. The page size is at most 20 and defaults to 20 (see GetDepositsOptions::size); it is always sent.

AP accounts only: US data-center accounts get a region error.

Path: GET /v1/account/deposits

Source

pub async fn withdrawals( &self, options: impl Into<Option<GetWithdrawalsOptions>>, ) -> Result<WithdrawalsResponse>

Get withdrawal records

WithdrawalsResponse::has_more reports a full page. The page size is at most 50 and defaults to 20 (see GetWithdrawalsOptions::size); it is always sent.

AP accounts only: US data-center accounts get a region error.

Path: GET /v1/account/withdrawals
